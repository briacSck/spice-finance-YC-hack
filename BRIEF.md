## The one-liner

YIELD plugs into an SMB's accounting + bank data in minutes, forecasts the cash hit **before it lands**, and **acts** — sweeps idle cash into yield, defends forecasted cash gaps, pays strategic suppliers early for a discount, pulls short credit — autonomously, settled on stablecoin rails where banks structurally can't (conditional, instant, 24/7, cross-border, no minimums).

**Onchain is not the pitch — it's the unfair rail.** It's what gives a €1M bakery the treasury operations only a Fortune 500 with a Goldman relationship gets today.

## The strategic call: a sharp wedge that *implies* a décacorn

The trap is stacking three giant ambitions — Anaplan (planning) + a Goldman quant desk (forecasting) + an onchain treasury (execution). To a YC partner that reads as "boil the ocean." The décacorn move is the inverse: **the sharpest wedge that implies the giant vision.** The vision is the iceberg; the demo is the tip that proves we can build the rest.

---

## The non-obvious insight

Everyone in 2026 is building "AI for the CFO." But look at what they ship: **read-only intelligence** — dashboards, forecasts, a chat box over the ledger. Agicap *shows* you a cash gap. ChatFin *tells* you about it. **They all stop at the glass.**

The reason is **structural, not a lack of ambition: bank rails can't execute autonomously.** You can't programmatically, conditionally, instantly move an SMB's money at 3am across a border on ACH/SEPA/SWIFT. So every incumbent hands the decision back to a human — the exact overwhelmed person drowning in Excel who has no time to act.

**The insight: the moment money becomes programmable (stablecoins, tokenized MMFs, RWA vaults — now real, MiCA-regulated, and a YC RFS), the bottleneck moves from *advice* to *execution* — and execution is where all the value and all the moat are.** The first company to close the loop (decision → action → settlement) autonomously, for the SMB that has no CFO to do it themselves, owns the category. Read-only tools become features of the thing that acts.

That's why it's a décacorn, not a unicorn: sit *between an SMB and its money flows* and own the *predictive graph of its receivables/payables/suppliers*, and you become (1) the system of record for SMB cash, (2) the best SMB credit underwriter alive — you see repayment capacity and supply-chain health no bank sees — and (3) a payments + yield + credit rail. TAM stops being "treasury SaaS seats" and becomes **a take-rate on the working-capital flows of every SMB in Europe.**

## The beachhead customer

**Mass-market EU SMBs with no real finance function.** Two shapes:
- **€1–5M micro/small business** — the bakery, the local distributor, the small e-com brand. The owner *is* the CFO and knows almost nothing about treasury or working capital.
- **€5–30M SMB with one overwhelmed "CFO"** doing payroll, VAT/URSSAF, and supplier payments in Excel — no time to optimize anything.

Why it wins:
- **Volume:** 3M+ SMBs in France, ~24M in the EU. Huge market, short sales cycle, turnkey B2B SaaS — not a 9-month enterprise deal.
- **Desperation:** they leave money on the table daily (idle cash at 0%, late receivables, missed discounts) and know it, but have no time and no expertise.
- **Turnkey = the moat:** "clé en main" — connect in 5 minutes, the AI does the rest. The less they know about finance, the more they need us and trust the agent to act.
- **The demo lands emotionally:** an AI giving a *bakery* a Fortune-500 treasury desk is visceral. The contrast is the wow.

## Why now (all true, no hype)

- **Stablecoin volume surpassed Visa in 2025.** Tokenized money-market funds: <$500M (2023) → **$7B+ AUM (Apr 2026)**. RWA yield vaults pay **4–13%** vs. ~0% in an SMB checking account.
- **YC is actively pulling for this.** Spring 2026 RFS: *"Stablecoin Financial Services."* YC now funds startups *in USDC*. **We hand the jury their own thesis, executed.**
- **Agentic finance crossed the line.** Production 13-week agentic forecasts hit **88–92% accuracy**; **52% of treasurers piloting AI** (≈2x in two years). Good enough to *trust with action*.
- **MiCA** gives EU regulatory clarity the US lacked — a structural edge for a France-first company.

---

## Moat

The **data flywheel.** Every SMB onboarded sharpens our forecast of receivable behavior and supplier health across the network — credit intelligence no bank can replicate because they don't see the supply chain. The execution layer compounds it: once we move the money, we're the system of record, and switching means handing cash operations back to Excel.

## Roadmap (wedge → décacorn)

1. **Now / Wedge:** turnkey AI CFO for EU SMBs — connect accounting + bank → forecast → autonomous idle-cash yield + early-payment discounts. Promise: *"we make your idle cash earn, and we never let you miss a cash gap."*
2. **Layer 2 — the brain deepens:** macro + supply-chain signals as *causal* inputs to the forecast (rates, sector, counterparty health). The forecast no one else has.
3. **Layer 3 — become the rail:** programmable credit (we underwrite better than banks — we see the flows), supplier finance, cross-border stablecoin payments. Take-rate on flows, not just SaaS seats — the décacorn line on the cap table.
4. **Endgame:** the system of record for SMB cash in Europe, and the credit graph beneath it.
