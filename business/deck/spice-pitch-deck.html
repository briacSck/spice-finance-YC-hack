01 — Insight & Problem
Three risks they never chose — and can't manage

Every small business is taking risks it never agreed to.

A bakery's costs swing with wheat and gas prices, its savings earn nothing, and a heatwave can wreck a season — three risks nobody puts in a spreadsheet. They show up as running out of cash.

Exposed to commodities

flour = wheat · diesel = oil · metal = steel/aluminium. 874 French bakeries went insolvent in 2022 — +124.7% YoY — after flour rose ~40% and energy spiked.

Cash at 0%

The buffer it must hold to survive a shock sits at zero yield. UK SME current-account cash grew £63.7B → £124B (2013–24) as inflation erodes it.

Exposed to weather

No protection against heat & footfall swings — no way to plan for it. 54 of 96 French departments hit red heat alert; construction output falls 29–41% under heat stress.

Nobody fixes this — hedging always needed a desk and a size these businesses don't have. Spice covers all three, autonomously, at SMB size.

Sources: Le Monde des Boulangers (2022 insolvencies) · INSEE (flour PPI) · UK Finance · Euronews · MDPI (heat-stress productivity) · World Bank

03 — Solution
Reads the books · forecasts the hit · hedges autonomously

Spice turns a P&L into a risk book
it can actually manage.

It decomposes the cost structure into the exposure underneath — "41% wheat, 22% energy" — forecasts the margin hit from historical commodity-price variation, then hedges across three venues, one per risk. No human spots the exposure, prices it, or calls a broker.

Input cost → Options

Calls on regulated commodity ETFs (wheat / gas / oil) via Alpaca. Caps the input-price spike.

Idle cash → Yield

Sweeps idle cash into on-chain lending (Aave / Morpho) instead of letting inflation erode it. Recallable.

Climate → Insurance

Parametric escrow pays out automatically when a price or weather index crosses a trigger — no adjuster, no claim.

A bakery, a trucking firm or a metalworker gets the same risk desk a Fortune 500 treasury has — in the background, settling in stablecoins, 24/7.
